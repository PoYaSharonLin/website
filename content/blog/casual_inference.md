# Casual Inference Overview

Casual inference is a statistical approach used to determine the cause-and-effect relationship between variables rather than the simple correlation. It is a powerful framework for understanding how interventions or treatments affect outcomes. 

The overall casual inference concepts can be covered by *The effect* by Nick Huntington-Klein (https://theeffectbook.net/index.html). Along the way, we will also cover some other references for each topic.
