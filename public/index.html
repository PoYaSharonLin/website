About

I am a Master’s student in Technology Management at National Tsing Hua University, specializing in AI-driven analytics and data-driven decision-making for business contexts. My interdisciplinary background bridges technical innovation with real-world economic impacts, particularly through the integration of user-generated content, social signals, and advanced natural language processing (NLP) techniques into predictive and platform-based systems.

A key contribution is my NSTC-funded publication on hybrid electric vehicle sales forecasting, where an ANFIS model incorporating electronic word-of-mouth (E-WOM) sentiment and volume features—extracted via NLP-driven text analysis—outperformed traditional approaches. This work highlights how online social data and review signals enhance sales prediction accuracy in dynamic markets, aligning with empirical investigations into the business value of user-generated content.

At Micron Technology, I developed internal tools using Autogen LLMs and Plotly dashboards for real-time semiconductor production analysis and forecasting, reducing query response times by 75% via optimized caching—demonstrating scalable AI applications that boost organizational productivity in high-stakes industries.

My open-source projects further explore digital platforms, user interactions, and NLP applications:

AcaRadar: A full-stack web app enabling interest-based retrieval of arXiv metadata through text embeddings and semantic search (leveraging NLP for personalized information discovery), architected with domain-driven design, RESTful APIs, and AWS SQS for resilient microservices—addressing challenges in cross-domain scholarly productivity and collective intelligence in academic platforms.

MindBigData: An interactive EEG classification tool deploying PyTorch models via Streamlit, facilitating real-time experimentation with multimodal human-AI interfaces.
