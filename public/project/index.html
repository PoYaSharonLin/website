Satellite Cloud Image prediction using ConvLSTM

• Applied ConvLSTM to predict the movement of the infrared cloud image and used MAE, MSE, SSIM, PSNR as performance evaluation.

• Compared to ConLSTM, Self-attention LSTM may not be as effective as expected due to the characteristics of cloud cluster movement. (only moves around one specific local area at 10-minute intervals.)
